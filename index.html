Getting started with DUKAPULSE

Sign in to your account and open your business workspace. Start by adding your products with their names, quantities, cost prices and selling prices. Then record sales and expenses as your business operates.

How do I record a sale?

Open the Sales area, select the products and quantities sold, choose the payment method and complete the sale. Check the sale summary before confirming. Recorded sales help update your stock and business performance figures.

How do I record business expenses?

Open Expenses, enter a clear description, choose a category, enter the amount and select the date. Save the record so it can be included in your expense history and profit calculations.

How do I manage stock and restocking?

Add products in Inventory and keep their quantities and prices accurate. When new stock arrives, use the available restocking feature. Set a low-stock threshold so the dashboard can highlight products that need attention.

Where can I see profit and reports?

Open Dashboard Analytics, Reports or Business Intelligence to review business performance. Profit calculations depend on the sales, product costs and expenses you have recorded. Keep records accurate for more useful results.

How do I manage customer debts?

Use the Debts area to record customer balances and due dates. When a customer pays, record the payment against the correct debt. DUKAPULSE uses recorded payments to calculate the remaining balance.

What if I cannot access my account?

Check that you are using the correct sign-in email. If you have forgotten your password, use the password recovery option on the login screen. You can also contact support for assistance.

Subscription and payment assistance

If you have a question about your trial, subscription or payment, contact support before making another payment. Never share your M-PESA PIN, password or one-time security codes with anyone.
